Lumina IPL Photo-Facial | nuCORE Photo-Rejuvenation Overview: Erase sun damage, fade stubborn dark spots, and restore luminous skin clarity. Powered by targeted, broad-spectrum intense pulsed light (IPL) technology on the advanced nuCORE platform, the Lumina Photo-Facial delivers light pulses that selectively absorb into excess melanin (pigment) and hemoglobin (vascular redness)—shattering unwanted skin discoloration while leaving surrounding healthy tissue completely unharmed. Treatable Indications: Hyperpigmentation & Sun Damage: Solar lentigines (sun spots), age spots, freckles, and benign dark patches Uneven Tone & Photo-Aging: Diffuse redness, mottled skin tone, and overall photo-damaged skin across the face, neck, chest, or hands Superficial Vascular Marks: Mild facial redness, fine broken capillaries, and early vascular flushing What to Expect During & After Treatment: Sensation: Feels like a series of quick, warm snaps against the skin (similar to a small rubber band). Integrated sapphire cooling ensures maximum patient comfort throughout the session. Immediate Response: Mild pinkness and subtle warmth post-treatment, similar to a light sun flush, which subsides within a few hours. Pigmented spots will temporarily darken—taking on a fine "coffee-ground" appearance—before naturally sloughing off over 5 to 7 days to reveal fresh, glowing skin. Downtime: Zero physical downtime. You may apply makeup immediately following your appointment and resume daily routines. Treatment Course & Results: Recommended Protocol: For comprehensive pigment clearance and skin tone transformation, a series of 3 to 4 sessions spaced 3 to 4 weeks apart is recommended. Results: Noticeably brighter, clearer, and more even skin tone within 1 to 2 weeks after your first session, with cumulative improvements over the series. Key Benefits: Non-invasive, non-ablative rejuvenation with zero skin-barrier breakdown Simultaneous targeting of brown spots and background red tones Fast 30-minute session time with immediate return to daily activities Physician-supervised, provider-directed clinical care Appointment Note: A clinical skin assessment is included with your visit. Please avoid direct sun exposure, tanning beds, self-tanning products, and photosensitizing medications for 2 to 4 weeks prior to treatment. Discontinue topical retinoids (Tretinoin, Retin-A) and AHA/BHA exfoliants 3 days prior.
