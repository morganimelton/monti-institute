Treatment Menu

What we offer

Every protocol is developed with your physician before your first appointment — dose, duration, and frequency calibrated to your neurology and goals.

01

Ketamine Infusion Therapy

Sub-anesthetic ketamine for treatment-resistant depression, anxiety, PTSD, and chronic pain. Protocols follow ASAM and AAPM guidelines — typically a series of six infusions over two to three weeks, with maintenance sessions as clinically indicated.

  • Depression
  • PTSD
  • Anxiety
  • Chronic Pain
  • OCD
02

Migraine & Headache Infusions

Multi-agent IV protocols for acute migraine abort and intractable headache — including DHE-45, magnesium sulfate, antiemetics, and hydration. Designed for patients who have not responded adequately to oral therapies or are in status migrainosus.

  • Acute Migraine
  • Cluster Headache
  • Status Migrainosus
  • Rebound Headache
03

IV Nutrient Therapy

High-dose intravenous micronutrient formulations — including Myers’ Cocktail, vitamin C, B-complex, zinc, and glutathione — for immune support, energy optimization, and recovery. Blends are physician-selected based on your lab panel and health goals.

  • Energy & Fatigue
  • Immune Support
  • Recovery
  • Hydration
04

NAD+ Infusions

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide administered intravenously to support cellular energy metabolism, cognitive clarity, and neurological resilience. Often used as an adjunct to ketamine protocols or alongside addiction recovery programs.

  • Cognitive Function
  • Cellular Recovery
  • Anti-Aging
  • Addiction Support
05

Custom & Compounded Drips

Physician-designed formulations prepared at an accredited compounding pharmacy — combining agents from multiple categories into a single infusion tailored to your presenting symptoms, labs, and treatment history. Developed in consultation only.

  • Physician-Designed
  • Lab-Guided
  • Multi-Agent
  • By Consultation
Your Experience

What to expect

I

Intake & consultation

A physician reviews your medical history, current medications, and goals. Lab work may be ordered before your first session. No infusion begins without a clinical clearance.

II

Protocol design

Your physician builds a custom infusion plan — selecting agents, dose, rate, and session cadence. For ketamine, integration support and preparation guidance are included.

III

Your infusion session

Administered in a private, low-stimulus suite. A clinician monitors vitals throughout. Sessions range from 45 minutes to four hours depending on the modality.

IV

Follow-up & continuity

After each session your care team reviews response and adjusts the plan as needed. Maintenance schedules are built around your clinical trajectory, not a fixed package.

The infusion suite is designed to disappear — quiet light, still air, no clinical noise. The only thing present is the therapy.
