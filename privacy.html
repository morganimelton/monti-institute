Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Effective Date: June 22, 2026

At the Monti Institute of Neuro Aesthetics ("we," "our," or "the Institute"), we are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our patients and digital visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, utilize, store, and safeguard your information across all communication vectors, including our website, electronic intake platforms, telephone calls, and Short Message Service (SMS) text messages.

1. Scope & HIPAA Alignment

This policy governs non-protected data collected through our digital marketing infrastructure, website tracking, and telecommunications.

Please note that Protected Health Information (PHI) collected during your formal clinical intake, medical consultations, or treatment history is securely maintained under separate, more stringent protocols governed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices is provided to all patients during clinical registration.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information through three primary channels to provide a highly personalized, elite aesthetic and wellness experience:

  • Internet & Website Data: When you visit our website, submit an online contact form, or use our digital concierge/AI chat features, we collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, and IP address. We also collect anonymized usage data through cookies to optimize your browsing experience.
  • Telephone Communications: Phone calls with our practice may be recorded or monitored for quality assurance, staff training, and clinical accuracy.
  • SMS & Mobile Text Data: When you text our clinic or opt-in to receive automated appointment reminders or marketing notifications, we collect your mobile phone number, carrier data, and the precise timestamp and content of your text exchanges.

3. How We Use Your Information

The Monti Institute utilizes your collected data strictly for operational, clinical, and communication purposes, including:

  • Confirming, updating, or modifying your scheduled appointments.
  • Answering inquiries submitted via our web portals or AI answering service.
  • Processing payments and managing account ledgers (e.g., Beauty Bank allocations).
  • Sending occasional practice updates, wellness newsletters, or exclusive member event invitations (where explicitly consented to).

4. SMS & Mobile Communication Protections (TCPA & CTIA Compliance)

We maintain strict boundaries regarding your mobile communication data. In compliance with wireless carrier compliance rules:

  • Explicit Opt-In Consent: By providing your mobile number and checking the consent box on our intake forms, you explicitly authorize the Monti Institute to send text messages (such as automated appointment reminders, prescription updates, and elective wellness offers) to your device. Consent to receive text messages is not a requirement to receive medical care at our facility.
  • The "No-Sharing" Mandate: We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your SMS opt-in consent data, phone numbers, or mobile information with any third-party marketing agencies, affiliates, or partners for their independent commercial gain. All text communications are managed strictly internally.
  • The Opt-Out Protocol: You retain total control over your mobile communications. You may completely opt-out of receiving text messages at any time by replying "STOP" to any text message received from our clinic. Upon receipt of a "STOP" command, your number will be permanently blacklisted from our text transmission infrastructure within 24 hours.

5. Third-Party Data Disclosures

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who are contractually bound by strict data-security clauses to assist in running our practice (such as our encrypted Electronic Health Record (EHR) software, our encrypted point-of-sale merchant processors, and our secure telecommunications providers).

We reserve the right to disclose personal data only if explicitly required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety and legal rights of our clinic, staff, or patients.

6. Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards—including end-to-end encryption and secure cloud-hosted firewalls—to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

7. Updates to This Policy

The Monti Institute of Neuro Aesthetics reserves the right to amend or update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changing technological infrastructure or shifts in regional telecommunication laws. The active policy will always feature the current version date at the top of this page.
