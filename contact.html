Premium Ground Transportation

Private Transportation & Car Services

For out-of-town patients arriving for multi-day treatment blocks, complex care consultations, or same-day infusion therapy, we recommend the following Northern Nevada private transportation providers. Each offers discreet, professional service consistent with the standard of care you will receive at the Institute.

Executive Car & Limousine

Executive Limousine of Reno

Reno’s premier executive ground transportation service, offering sedan, SUV, and stretch limousine options for airport arrivals, hotel transfers, and multi-stop care days. Preferred by visiting executives and medical travelers throughout the Northern Nevada region.

Advance reservation recommended for airport-to-practice transfers.

Regional Car Service

Reno Tahoe Limousine

Full-service limousine and car service covering the Reno–Tahoe corridor, with particular expertise in Reno-Tahoe International Airport pickups, South Lake Tahoe transfers, and multi-day patient itineraries across both mountain and metropolitan routes.

Ideal for patients traveling from the Lake Tahoe Basin for extended treatment visits.

Private Charter & Luxury Ground

Sunset Limousine

A well-regarded regional ground transportation provider with a fleet of premium vehicles available for airport arrivals, corporate transfers, and door-to-door practice coordination. Reliable, discreet, and familiar with the Northern Nevada medical travel corridor.

Available for hourly booking on treatment days with multiple appointment blocks.

On-Demand Premium Rideshare

Uber Black & Lyft Premium

Both Uber Black and Lyft Premium are available throughout the greater Reno metro area and drop directly at our front entrance at 2440 Vassar Street, Suite 3. For patients who prefer on-demand flexibility over scheduled car service, these options offer professional, late-model vehicles with no advance reservation required.

Select “Uber Black” or “Lyft Premium” within your respective app for the premium vehicle and driver tier.

Concierge coordination note: For patients coordinating multi-day visits, our Director of Operations can assist in aligning transportation logistics with your appointment schedule. Contact us at admin@monti-institute.com to arrange itinerary support.
